# T47 preregistration: blind test of the cotton momentum rule on 1985–1999 USDA forecasts

Written 16 Nov 2026, before any 1985–1999 USDA cotton forecast or final was downloaded or loaded. Scoring runs once per stage, after this file is pushed.

## Why
The cotton momentum record (2002–2025) is descriptive: 2002–2011 were blind, 2012–2025 were seen. T46 showed the rule does not carry over to corn or soybeans. The remaining clean test is cotton seasons nobody here has looked at: USDA Crop Production reports 1985–1999.

## Data
- **Stage A, 1995–1999:** ESMIS Crop Production text reports, Aug–Dec (25 reports), parsed with the unchanged T45 parser `cp_pull.py` (upland block, last number = current forecast, 1,000 bales).
- **Stage B, 1985–1994:** the same reports exist only as scanned PDFs (no text layer). They will be OCR'd (Tesseract, run on a Kaggle CPU kernel; nothing is installed on this machine). Stage B is attempted whatever stage A shows.
- **Finals:** NASS QuickStats upland production, `YEAR` rows (`t47_blind_old.py finals`), the same definition as every earlier trial.
- **Gate (outcome-blind; uses no finals):** a report is kept only if it has a TX row and a US row and the sum of its state rows is within 2% of its US row. Failing reports are listed and dropped before scoring. For stage B, OCR rows must also pass the same check per report.

## Rule (frozen)
- **Primary, both stages:** momentum coefficients b and σ per report month fitted once on 2000–2025 state data. The formula is `lock_2026.coef`, reproduced in `t47_blind_old.coef`; it gives the same Nov/Dec values as the 2026 lock. These coefficients are applied unchanged: ê = b · log(USDA_m / USDA_prev) / σ, forecast = USDA_m · exp(ê); US = USDA_US × Σ corrected / Σ USDA.
- **Coefficients, disclosed now** (computed from 2000–2025 only):

  | Month | b | σ |
  |---|---|---|
  | Sep | −0.01055 | 0.1338 |
  | Oct | +0.01866 | 0.0759 |
  | Nov | +0.01011 | 0.0830 |
  | Dec | −0.00567 | 0.0732 |

  Over the full 2000–2025 sample, the Sep and Dec coefficients are small and negative. The rolling record's Sep and Dec gains therefore came from coefficients that changed over time. The frozen test is a test of these small pooled coefficients.
- **Secondary, stage B only:** the original rolling procedure (`momentum.py`, trained on seasons ≥ 1985 and < Y), scored 1987–1999 with `COTTONLENS_MOMENTUM_FIRST_TRAIN=1985`.

## Metrics and pass rule
- Per month (Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec):
  - TX and US MAPE of the rule vs USDA, with years better / worse and a one-sided sign-flip p;
  - production-weighted state error of the rule vs USDA.
- **PRIMARY PASS (per stage, and for A+B combined):** the rule's MAPE is below USDA's in at least 6 of the 8 TX/US cells **and** its state-weighted error is below USDA's in at least 3 of the 4 months.
- **Also reported:** a year-block permutation p pooled over the 8 cells. With 5 seasons (stage A) the smallest possible p is 1/32, so stage A alone cannot be significant. Stage A is a direction check; significance needs stage B.
- The results are reported in full whatever they show: `research/evidence/t47-*.json`, `docs/CROP_NOWCAST_TRIALS.md`, the package and the portfolio page.

## Disclosures
- Before writing this file, `get_counts` showed that QuickStats has 1985–1999 upland finals (row counts only). ESMIS listings showed which months have txt or pdf files. Two scanned PDFs (Sep 1985, Sep 1990) were downloaded to test for a text layer; they have none, and no value was read.
- The coefficients above use only 2000–2025 data, which was already seen.
