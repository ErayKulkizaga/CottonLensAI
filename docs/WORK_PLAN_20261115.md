# CottonLensAI work plan (15 Nov 2026), waiting for the user's approval

The single ordered plan, gathered from all instructions given so far. All trials are logged in [CROP_NOWCAST_TRIALS.md](CROP_NOWCAST_TRIALS.md).

## Rules for every track
- **One track at a time.** One track is active; the others wait in the queue.
- **Track setup.** Each track has its own branch, its own folder, a gate written down before any results are seen, and rows in the trial ledger.
- **Goal.** The goal is to achieve, not to lower the target. When a method fails, the next one is tried at once. Methods are combined in ensembles.
- **Sealed years 2022–2025.** They are opened once, only after a preregistration is pushed and the user gives an explicit yes.
- **Reproducibility.** Results come from the pinned environment (sklearn 1.6.1, pandas 2.2.3). `docs/CLAUDE_HANDOFF.md` is never committed.

- **Branch:** `claude/hls-move3`. **Folder:** `research/crop_nowcast/`.
- **Status:**
  - At 1 Sep and 1 Oct, USDA is beaten only by a bias correction (T25). It depends on USDA's upward bias persisting.

  - 1 Aug is not beaten.
- **Next attempts (1 Aug first):**
  1. County-level FSA failed share combined with the satellite county model.
  2. FSA failed share split into irrigated and dryland.
  3. FSA planted area in place of the June Acreage figure.
  4. An ensemble of the survivors.
- **Gate for the sealed test:** the method must
  - beat USDA on 2013–2021;
  - win in most years;
  - beat the matched bias model T25.

  If it passes, a preregistration is written, the user approves, it is pushed, and the sealed years are opened once.

## Track 2 (queued): GPU deep satellite model, feeding Track 1
- **Branch:** `claude/gpu-deep`. **Folder:** `research/deep/`.
- **Why:** each county is currently reduced to a few NDVI numbers, so field shape, timing and texture are lost.
- **Order:**
  1. Prithvi-EO-2.0 embeddings of Landsat field images, added as features to the county model. This is a pre-declared trial for TX at 1 Aug. Not verified: whether Prithvi works with Landsat C2 bands, since it was trained on HLS.
  2. Histogram + CNN/LSTM on the season's time series (You et al. 2017).
  3. Multi-seed and multi-model ensemble.
- **Platform:** start on the Kaggle GPU (free weekly quota, driven by the CLI). When the quota runs out, continue in Colab (A100/L4, Drive `G:\Drive'ım`) without starting over:
  - The same script detects the platform (`/kaggle/working` or `/content`).
  - The work is split into units (state × year, or a training epoch block).
  - A `manifest.json` records each unit as done or pending, with its sha256.
  - Finished units and checkpoints go to a shared store: a Kaggle dataset version mirrored to Drive.
  - A finished unit is never recomputed, and training resumes from the last checkpoint.
  - Size and sync limits of that store are to be checked first.
- **Gate:** the Track 1 harness. A gain is counted only if it beats the best existing Track 1 model on the same years.
